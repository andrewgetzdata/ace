*Advise · Coach · Execute*

**v1 — May 2026** · For walkthrough with Roy

# The Lifecycle at a Glance

Five touchpoints, mapped left to right from first contact through ongoing engagement. Each has a clear purpose, audience, format, and exit criteria. The further right, the warmer and more committed the prospect.

| Stage | Length | Audience | Cost to prospect | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Qualifying Call** | 30 min | Owner or Integrator only | Free | Mutual fit decision · go / no-go to pitch |
| **2. Pitch Meeting** | 90 min | Leadership team | Free | Value-driven walkthrough · tier recommendation · go / no-go to discovery |
| **3a. Executive Discovery** | Half-day · AM scope, PM Rock | Leadership team only | Paid · standalone fixed-fee | One scoped Rock ready for next L10 · written analysis |
| **3b. Operator Discovery** | Full day · AM scope, PM Rock + frontline workshop | Leadership + frontline / department heads | Paid · premium fixed-fee | Same as 3a + frontline buy-in & workflow mapping |
| **4. Tiered Engagement** | Ongoing | Per tier (Advise / Coach / Execute) | Recurring or fixed-fee | Sustained AI work tied to client's EOS rhythm |

## Stage 1 · Qualifying Call (30 min)

### Purpose

- Both sides confirm there's enough fit to invest more time
- Disqualify quickly if the prospect isn't running an operating system, is too small, or wants a platform sale
- Set up the pitch meeting if there's a real opportunity

### Audience

- Owner or Integrator only (not the full leadership team)
- One person who can say yes to a follow-up

### Cost

- Free, scheduled via calendar link
- 30 minutes maximum — hard stop

### What ACE delivers

- Two-sentence ACE positioning ("judgment over tooling, three tiers")
- Two qualifying questions about their current AI conversation and operating cadence
- Honest read on whether to schedule the 90-min pitch

### Exit criteria

- **Go:** Schedule the pitch meeting with leadership team
- **No-go:** Decline kindly, offer a referral if relevant

## Stage 2 · Pitch Meeting (90 min)

*Modeled on the EOS 90-min introductory meeting structure.*

### Purpose

- Drive home the value of working with ACE
- Show the leadership team how ACE thinks about AI for their kind of business
- Earn the right to a paid discovery

### Audience

- Full leadership team — Visionary, Integrator, all leaders on the Accountability Chart
- Why full team: AI decisions cross seats; one person can't carry the weight

### Cost

- Free
- Investment: 90 minutes of leadership time, ACE's preparation

### What ACE delivers (the value engine)

- The four-question filter — taught, not described — so the team leaves with a usable framework
- Three real stories matched to their industry / size / tension
- An "AI Horizon" framing exercise applied lightly to their stated 1-year plan
- Tier recommendation with reasoning
- Honest assessment of whether discovery is the right next step

### What's NOT in the pitch

- No deep dive into their actual data or workflows (that's discovery)
- No vendor-pitch filtering on their real situation (that's discovery)
- No Rock scoping (that's discovery)
- No pricing pressure

### Exit criteria

- **Go:** Scope a paid discovery — Executive (half-day) or Operator (full day)
- **No-go:** Refer to AI 101 standalone session, or recommend they wait

## Stage 3 · Discovery (paid, EOS-aligned)

*Positioned as a "90-day Rock kickoff add-on" to the client's existing EOS rhythm. Slot it in before a quarterly offsite. By end of session, leadership has at least one scoped AI Rock ready to take to their next L10 or quarterly meeting.*

### Why two versions

- Different audiences require different depth and time
- Roy's principle: don't mix leadership-level and frontline-level in the same session
- Operator version costs more because it's a longer day with a workshop component for frontline staff

### Stage 3a · Executive Discovery (half-day)

#### Purpose

- Strategic-level: where does AI fit in the leadership team's plan
- Output one scoped AI Rock with named owner, budget, kill criterion, and 90-day done definition
- Identify whether the Rock falls into Coach or Execute work

#### Audience

- Leadership team only — Visionary, Integrator, and seat owners

#### Format

- Half-day · 4 hours total
- **AM block · Scope (2 hrs):** AI level-set, current state assessment, opportunity mapping, four-question filter applied to live candidates
- **PM block · Rock (2 hrs):** Pick the strongest candidate, complete the Rock one-pager, plan the first 30 days, identify needed resources

#### Cost

- Fixed-fee · target $2,500–3,500
- Credits 100% toward Coach or Execute engagement if signed within 30 days

#### Deliverables

- Written analysis: current AI/data state, top 3 opportunities ranked, risks identified
- One scoped AI Rock — ready to drop into the next L10 or quarterly
- Recommended tier and entry point

### Stage 3b · Operator Discovery (full day)

#### Purpose

- Same as Executive, plus frontline buy-in and workflow mapping
- Avoid the "great Rock that frontline rejects" failure mode
- Produce a Rock that's been pressure-tested by the people who'll execute it

#### Audience

- Leadership team in AM
- Frontline / department heads in PM (overlap with leadership)

#### Format

- Full day · ~7 hours with lunch
- **AM block · Executive Scope (3 hrs):** Same as Stage 3a AM block
- **Lunch · 1 hr**
- **PM block 1 · Frontline Workshop (2 hrs):** Walk frontline through the candidate Rock, map current workflow, identify friction and risk, refine
- **PM block 2 · Rock Finalization (1 hr):** Leadership reconvenes, finalize Rock with frontline input baked in

#### Cost

- Fixed-fee · target $4,500–6,000
- Credits 100% toward Coach or Execute engagement if signed within 30 days

#### Deliverables

- Same as Executive Discovery
- Plus: workflow map of the AI-touched process — trigger, AI's job, human checkpoint, fallback, kill switch
- Plus: frontline-validated implementation plan

## Stage 4 · Tiered Engagement (ongoing)

*The lifecycle endpoint. After a successful Discovery, prospects roll into one of three tiers based on the work the Rock requires.*

### Advise · ~$1.2–1.5K/mo

- Outside thinking only — no implementation
- ~2 hrs/week
- Best when there's no immediate AI Rock but pressure is building

### Coach · ~$3K/mo

- Configuring existing tools — Claude Projects, Custom GPTs, vendor SaaS
- ~5 hrs/week
- Best when the discovery Rock is solvable with existing tools well-applied

### Execute · $10–40K per project

- Custom engineering — novel systems, hosted, real code
- Fixed-fee, 6–12 weeks
- Best when the discovery Rock genuinely requires building something new

## How This Maps to EOS

The lifecycle is designed to slot into an EOS-running company without disrupting their cadence. Each ACE stage maps to a corresponding EOS rhythm:

| ACE Stage | EOS Equivalent | How it slots in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifying Call | Pre-meeting introduction | Owner / Integrator initial conversation; no committee involvement |
| Pitch Meeting (90 min) | EOS Intro Meeting (the "90-Minute Meeting") | Direct parallel — same time box, same audience structure, same value-first principle |
| Discovery (half/full day) | Quarterly Pulsing or Annual Planning add-on | Slotted before the next quarterly to produce a Rock for that quarter |
| Tiered Engagement | Ongoing L10s, quarterly Rocks, annual planning | ACE attends specific L10s (Coach), provides quarterly AI input (Advise), or executes between cadences (Execute) |

### The integrator's experience

From the Integrator's perspective, ACE is not a new system to learn. It's an outside resource that speaks EOS, attends the meetings they already run, and produces outputs in the formats they already use (Rocks, Issues, Scorecard items). The lower the friction to adopt, the higher the conversion.

## Open Questions for the Walkthrough

Items to pressure-test with Roy:

- **Qualifying Call format.** Is 30 minutes the right length, or should it be 15? Does it need a structured agenda or stay loose?
- **Pitch Meeting value depth.** Where's the line between "give enough value to earn the next step" and "give so much that they don't need to hire you"? The four-question filter is the obvious teaching moment — what else?
- **Discovery pricing.** Are $2,500–3,500 (Executive) and $4,500–6,000 (Operator) the right anchors? Too high to land first clients, or too low to signal value?
- **Discovery credit-back.** Does crediting 100% toward an ongoing engagement work, or does it commoditize the Discovery itself? Alternative: 50% credit, or no credit but lower Discovery price.
- **Operator Discovery viability.** Is a full day too long for SMB leadership to commit on a first paid engagement? Or is the day-long format actually the strongest signal of seriousness?
- **Sequencing.** Should every prospect be required to do a Discovery before any tiered engagement, or can warm prospects (Kelly, Dave, Nader) skip directly to Coach if the Rock is obvious?
- **Standalone Discovery.** Should Discovery be available without a Pitch Meeting first, for prospects who already know they want to engage?
- **Naming.** "Executive Discovery" vs. "Operator Discovery" — clear enough? Or does the language suggest a hierarchy that creates pricing pressure (every prospect wants the "executive" tier)?
- **EOS module positioning.** Should this be marketed to EOS Implementers as a referable add-on module — and if so, does Roy's network unlock that?

Free 30-min qualifying call → free 90-min value-driven pitch with leadership → paid half-day or full-day Discovery that produces a real Rock → ongoing engagement at one of three tiers, mapped to the client's existing EOS rhythm.
